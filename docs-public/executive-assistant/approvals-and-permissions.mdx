# Approvals And Permissions

Executive Assistant is designed around explicit approval for risky or externally visible work.

## Approval examples

- Send or schedule a message.
- Make a commitment on behalf of the user.
- Change provider permissions.
- Add or remove a delivery channel.
- Resolve a decision that affects another person.

## Permission examples

- Gmail and Calendar access through OAuth.
- Telegram or WhatsApp channel binding.
- Notification delivery preferences.
- Retention and export settings.

## Safe default

If the system is uncertain whether an action is sensitive, it should ask for approval instead of proceeding silently.
