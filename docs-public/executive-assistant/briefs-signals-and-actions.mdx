# Briefs, Signals And Actions

Executive Assistant separates raw activity from useful work.

## Briefs

A brief is a short, evidence-backed summary of what changed, what matters, and what decision is needed next.

## Signals

A signal is an event that might matter: a new email, changed meeting, stale commitment, unanswered request, or channel message.

## Actions

An action is work the system can prepare for review, such as a draft reply, a follow-up reminder, a handoff, or a queue resolution.

## Quality bar

Every important item should answer why it is present, what evidence supports it, what changed, who needs to approve it, and what happens if it is ignored.
