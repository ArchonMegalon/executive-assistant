# Workspaces And Identity

An Executive Assistant workspace binds one executive identity to one controlled operating context. The workspace is the boundary for connected providers, delivery channels, approval rules, and retention settings.

## Roles

- Executive: owns the account, connected providers, decisions, and approvals.
- Operator: helps review briefs, queue items, and setup posture when explicitly allowed.
- System: prepares structured briefs and draft actions within the configured permissions.

## Identity model

Registration starts with email verification. Provider connections are attached only after the user confirms the provider flow. A workspace should not share provider connections across unrelated executives.

## Access hygiene

Use separate accounts for testing and production. Revoke provider access if a device, email account, or channel binding is no longer trusted.
