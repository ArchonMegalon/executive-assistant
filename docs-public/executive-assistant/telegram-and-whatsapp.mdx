# Telegram And WhatsApp

Telegram and WhatsApp are delivery channels for briefs, approvals, reminders, and status updates. They are not the source of truth for account identity.

## Telegram

Telegram is best for fast operator-style updates and low-friction approvals. Link the bot only from a trusted chat, and remove the binding if the chat changes ownership.

## WhatsApp

WhatsApp is best when the user already treats it as the practical command channel. Setup may involve a business account or import/export path depending on the deployment.

## Channel safety

Do not rely on channel possession alone for sensitive changes. High-impact actions should still require approval through the configured policy.
