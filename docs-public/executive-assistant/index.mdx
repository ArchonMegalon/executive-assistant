# Executive Assistant

Executive Assistant is a private chief-of-staff system for one executive and one trusted operator. It turns connected inbox, calendar, Telegram, WhatsApp, and approval signals into a daily brief, a decision queue, a commitment ledger, and reviewable actions.

The public docs explain what the product does, how setup works, what permissions are requested, and how to troubleshoot common connection issues. They do not expose internal runtime routes, provider repair tools, operator-only prompts, private records, or deployment details.

## What Executive Assistant helps with

- A morning brief that shows what changed, what needs a decision, and what can wait.
- Signals from Gmail, Calendar, Telegram, and WhatsApp that are grouped by actionability.
- Approval-bound drafts for messages, follow-ups, and handoffs.
- A commitment ledger that keeps promises, deadlines, and pending replies visible.
- Evidence-backed troubleshooting for identity, permissions, and channel setup.

## What stays outside the public docs

- Credentials, tokens, provider internals, and recovery material.
- Private messages, private documents, and decision history.
- Security runbooks, incident records, and internal escalation procedures.
- Fleet, model-routing, CodexEA, and operator-only automation surfaces.
