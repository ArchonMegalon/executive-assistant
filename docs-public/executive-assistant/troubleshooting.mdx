# Troubleshooting

Most setup issues come from identity mismatch, provider consent, channel binding, or stale browser sessions.

## Registration

If registration does not complete, verify that the email address is correct, the link has not expired, and the browser is not blocking the sign-in flow.

## Google connection

If Gmail or Calendar connection fails, sign out of unrelated Google profiles, retry the OAuth flow, and confirm the requested scopes match the enabled features.

## Telegram

If Telegram does not link, restart the bot flow from the intended chat and confirm that the chat is not a forwarded or archived conversation.

## WhatsApp

If WhatsApp setup stalls, confirm that the intended phone number and business setup path are being used. Do not copy setup codes into untrusted chats.

## Approvals

If an action appears stuck, check whether it is waiting for approval, missing provider permission, or blocked by a delivery channel issue.
