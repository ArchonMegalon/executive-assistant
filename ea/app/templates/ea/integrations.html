Integrations

Connect only what improves the office loop.

Start with the narrowest setup that makes return access, delivery, and signal sync work. For most offices that means email-first account creation and optional Google identity, not a broad integration bundle.

Recommended order
Google only if it helps return access

Google should mainly improve sign-in continuity and explicit office sync. It should not force broad mailbox or calendar scope into the first office workflow unless the workspace is ready for it.

Know what each connection can do

Each connection should say clearly what Executive Assistant can identify, draft, notify, or sync today. Trust drops when the UI implies more than the contract really allows.

GoogleBest when the same Google identity should reopen the workspace cleanly and later support inbox, calendar, and delivery workflows with explicit review boundaries.
Email notificationsBest when approvals, summaries, and reviewed drafts should be delivered asynchronously without turning email into hidden automation.
Future delivery lanesBest when later premium workflows justify more channels, not before.
