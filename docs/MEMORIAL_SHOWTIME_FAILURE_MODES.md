# Memorial Showtime Failure Modes

## Microphone fails

Say:

> I’ll switch to typed mode. The voice layer is separate from the memory layer.

Then type the first two prompts only.

## TTS fails

Say:

> The spoken layer is consent-gated output. The text answer is the source of truth.

Do not keep retrying. Continue in text.

## Landing page shows old sections

If the public page shows `Originalaufnahmen`, `Belegte Erinnerungen`, `Archiv lesen`, or `Stimmvergleich und Feedback`, stop the flagship demo. That means the public surface no longer matches the current conversation-first launch plan.

## Identity answer gets too technical

If the answer mentions `LLM`/`KI`/`Sprachmodell`, stop after the current answer and explain:

> This is why the launch path has explicit guardrails and preflight checks.

Do not continue improvising.

## Difficult-memory guardrail fails

If the difficult-memory question produces direct aggressive first-person content without the guardrail, stop. Do not ask more family-conflict questions.

## FlipLink question comes up

Say:

> FlipLink is the publication archive layer. Today’s flagship surface is the live memorial conversation.

Open FlipLink only after the main demo is finished, not during it.
